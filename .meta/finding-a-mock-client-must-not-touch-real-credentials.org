#+TITLE: A mock client must not touch real credentials

* Incident
=wave-ui--password= fell back to =auth-source-search= for a mock server.
On first batch run it began decrypting the developer's real
=~/.authinfo.gpg= and blocked on the gpg passphrase. Separately, the 2010
client's default backend tried to log in at google.com when a wave opened.

* Shape
Convenience defaults inherited from a real-world client (auth-source,
legacy backends) reach real credentials and real services from a sandbox
tool, and in batch they hang instead of failing.

* Do instead
- Make credential-store lookups opt-in for mock/dev clients.
- Bind =auth-sources= to nil in batch test drivers as a second guard.
- Force the backend you mean; don't inherit a legacy default.
